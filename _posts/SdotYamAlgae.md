###Inroduction
Diverse species of algae were sampled from the rocky shallow reefs of the coast of Sdot Yam from light and dark habitats. These were then taken into the lab and photophysiology parameteres were measured by PAM (Pulse Amplitude Modulation). The goal of this excersize is to determine how photophysiology parameters differ between light and dark habitat algae.

###Methods
Algae samples from Sdot Yam froh dark habitats came from under shaded areas of rocky pools where no light was visible contacting them, while lighy habitat algae came from areas completely exposed to sunlight without anu shading. These sampling were extracted by hand and sealed in plastic bags to transport bacl to the lab. These were then sorted to find optimally sized algae pieces to anaylse by PAM.

Parameters analyzed include Y(II) (Effective Quantum Yield of PSII), NPQ (Non-Photochemical Quenching), Fv/Fm (Photosystem II maximum potential quantum efficiency) and ETR (Electron Transport Rate). THese paramaetrs were all computed by the PAM, except for Fv/Fm which was calculated from PAM-derived parameters Fo (Minimum Fluorescence) and Fm (Maximum Fluorescence)  using the Fv/Fm equation ([Fm-Fo]/Fm). The paraemeters Y(II), NPQ, Fv/Fm, and ETR were measured for each algad Taxon, with actual photsynthesis patameters Am (asymptotic maximum = Pmax= max photosynthesis), AQY (initial sloap = alpha = Fv/Fm), Rd (curve y intercept = dark respiration (normalized oxygen = positive values [usually negative]), and Ik (Am/AQY, light-limited to saturation-limited irradiance level) determined on average.
